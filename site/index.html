The Three Tenets

What we stand for — without compromise.

I.
5:30 at the athletic facility
Arrival before the sun. No excuses, no snooze, no exceptions. The day is won before most of the world has turned over.
II.
Chocolate milk
The recovery drink of record. Consumed with reverence. A reminder that the best things are often the simplest.
III.
Actively chopping it up
Conversation is currency. We show up, we speak up, we keep the room alive. The silent need not apply.
Our Standard

The qualities every member is expected to embody — and demonstrate, daily.

Character

  • Consistency — in training, in conduct, in word
  • Dedication beyond the visible
  • Alignment with all three tenets

Pursuit

  • Gymnastics or equivalent athletic rigor
  • Command of a musical instrument
  • Demonstrable skill at video games

Academic

  • Unweighted GPA above 3.8
  • Submitted ACT or SAT

Voice

  • Willing to defend a hot take
  • Able to host a table
  • Present — wherever present is required
