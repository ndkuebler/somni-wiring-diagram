Admission is limited. Standards are not. What follows is the complete application — read carefully, answer truthfully, and submit only if the three tenets feel less like rules and more like how you already live.

The Three Tenets

Non-negotiable. These are not preferences — they are the floor.

I.
5:30 at the athletic facility
Arrival before the sun. No excuses, no snooze, no exceptions.
II.
Chocolate milk
The recovery drink of record. Consumed with reverence.
III.
Actively chopping it up
Conversation is currency. The silent need not apply.
Eligibility

What every candidate must demonstrate before being considered.

Character

  • Alignment with all three tenets
  • Consistency — in training, in conduct, in word
  • Dedication beyond the visible

Extracurricular

  • Gymnastics
  • Command of a musical instrument
  • Demonstrable skill at video games

Academic

  • Unweighted GPA above 3.8
  • Submission of ACT or SAT score

Written

  • Personal essay
  • One 100-word response
  • Two single-sentence supplementals
The Application

Answer with the care the questions deserve.

Written Portion
I.  Personal Essay
No word limit
Tell us who you are — in the shape and length you believe the answer requires.
II.  The 100
100 words
Describe a moment that proves you live by the three tenets — not just observe them.
III.  Supplemental
One sentence
What is your hottest take?
IV.  Supplemental
One sentence
What is your perfect dinner, and why?
